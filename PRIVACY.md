### 웹판 방문 통계

이 절은 웹판(https://tinkerer0.github.io/color-war/)에만 해당하며 아이폰 앱에는 해당하지 않습니다. 웹판은 방문자 수를 알기 위해 GoatCounter를 씁니다. GoatCounter는 방문한 페이지 주소, 이전 페이지(리퍼러), 브라우저·운영체제 종류, IP 주소로 추정한 국가, 언어, 화면 너비를 시간·날짜별 합계로 집계합니다. 쿠키나 브라우저 저장 공간을 쓰지 않고, IP 주소를 저장하지 않습니다. 같은 방문을 두 번 세지 않도록 IP 주소와 브라우저 정보를 최대 8시간 동안 메모리에서만 대조합니다. 자세한 내용은 [GoatCounter 개인정보 처리방침](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy)을 참고해 주세요. 광고 차단기 등으로 통계 스크립트를 막아도 웹판은 똑같이 동작합니다.

### Information collected

Color War Simulator does not collect personal information. There is no account or sign-in, and the app does not ask for or read your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers. It does not request access to the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos. For visit statistics on the web version, see "Web version visit statistics" below.

### Information stored on your device

Only settings (time limit, sound, vibration) are stored, in the app's storage on this device. They are not sent to anyone, including the developer, and are removed when you delete the app. Apple device backup and restore follow your device settings and Apple's policies.

### Network and third parties

The app does not connect to the internet and uses no advertising, analytics, payment or tracking tools. Tapping "Privacy Policy" (Korean: "개인정보 처리방침") in Settings opens this document in Safari, where GitHub's privacy policy applies.

### Web version visit statistics

This section applies only to the web version (https://tinkerer0.github.io/color-war/), not to the iPhone app. The web version uses GoatCounter to count visits. GoatCounter aggregates, per hour or day, the page address, the previous page (referrer), browser and operating system type, country derived from the IP address, language and screen width. It does not use cookies or browser storage and does not store IP addresses. To avoid counting the same visit twice, it matches the IP address and browser information in memory only, for up to eight hours. See the [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for details. The web version works the same if you block the statistics script, for example with an ad blocker.

### Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
